# Lazarus Moodles

A Forge 1.20.1 HUD mod for Project Lazarus. Each player has one three-stage fear moodle (Tense → Fearful → Panicked), plus independent Pain, Exhausted, and Relieved moodles.

The fear moodle rises through its stages as nearby monsters, darkness, low health, and damage increase pressure. Only one fear stage appears at a time. Pain is a separate moodle derived from current health: level 1 at 80% health or less, level 2 at 50% or less, and level 3 at 25% or less. Each hit adds acute pain, raising Pain one level (up to 3) for 5 seconds. Painkillers (Legendary Survival Overhaul morphine `painkiller` or Apocalypse Now `painkillerseffect`) lower Pain by 2 levels while active. Exhausted gains one level per 1,000 blocks traveled on foot (walking, sprinting, or crouching), up to level 5. It never decays on its own. Sleeping through the night resets it, and so do two minutes in bed even if the night is not skipped (common on servers). Resting crouched and completely still while not afraid lowers it by one level per minute, down to level 2; only sleep goes lower. Vehicles, swimming, and teleports do not add distance. Relieved appears after escaping nearby monsters, and grows with how scared you were: escaping while Tense, Fearful, or Panicked adds 20, 40, or 70 relief points (levels start at 15/35/60/85). Escaping without fear gives no relief. Only fear that rose during the threat counts, so a monster drifting in and out of range while fear is already fading does not grant relief again. Relief fades by 2 points per second, or 8 while monsters are near. Fear, exhaustion, and relief persist with the player and synchronize from the server to that player's HUD; Pain is recalculated from health. Existing saves migrate their strongest active fear moodle to the new scale, capped at Panicked. Old sprint-based Exhausted levels restart at zero when upgrading to distance-based Exhausted.

## Gameplay effects

- Fear adds food and Legendary Survival Overhaul thirst exhaustion each second. Tense, Fearful, and Panicked add 0.02/0.05/0.10 food exhaustion and 0.012/0.03/0.06 thirst exhaustion respectively. Aiming with TaCZ or vanilla ranged weapons causes aim sway of about 0.35°/0.9°/1.8° per stage; Fearful and Panicked add a fast hand tremor, and the sway grows after sprinting or taking damage.
- Fear also slows TaCZ reloads on the server: reloads take about 1.10x/1.25x/1.45x as long, so ammo is loaded and firing resumes later. The TaCZ reload animation itself plays at normal speed.
- Fearful plays a heartbeat at about 86 bpm and heavy breathing every 6–12 seconds. Panicked raises the heartbeat to about 133 bpm, and louder breathing returns every 3.5–6.5 seconds. Each breath picks one of four recorded takes with slightly varied pitch and volume, so it does not sound like a loop. Both sounds were generated with ElevenLabs and play in the Master channel.
- The screen edges darken with a blood-red vignette that grows with each stage and fades in and out gradually. From Fearful on, it throbs with each heartbeat. Panicked adds a faint red flash on each beat, a slight tunnel-vision FOV squeeze, and a trembling camera roll. The vignette, flash, and roll scale with the vanilla Distortion Effects slider, and the FOV squeeze scales with FOV Effects.
- Pain reduces melee attack speed by 4/8/12% and PeakStamina regeneration by 5/10/15%. It also reduces forward input while climbing ladders or vines by 8/16/24%.
- Every hit gives pain feedback: a grunt (four takes with varied pitch, louder at higher Pain and for bigger hits; small repeated damage such as bleeding grunts only sometimes), a short camera flinch away from the hit, and a brief pale flash. A hit of 3 or more damage at Pain 3 also makes the ears ring for about 4 seconds, at most once every 15 seconds. The flinch moves only the camera, not the aim. The flinch and flash scale with the vanilla Distortion Effects slider. Painkillers suppress all hit feedback.
- Exhausted reduces PeakStamina regeneration by 5% per level and increases sprint stamina cost by 6% per level. From level 3, jump and attack stamina costs rise by 10/20/30%. At levels 4 and 5, movement speed falls by 3/6%, maximum stamina falls by 10/20%, aim sways by an extra 0.3°/0.5°, and food and thirst drain like Tense/Fearful fear (0.02/0.04 food and 0.012/0.024 thirst exhaustion per second, stacking with fear).
- From Exhausted 3, the eyes close in slow blinks (every 20–40 s at level 3, 10–25 s at level 4, 6–15 s at level 5) and the character yawns every 1–2 min (level 3), 45–90 s (level 4), or 30–60 s (level 5), using three recorded takes. Yawning stops while Fearful or Panicked. From level 4, stopping after sprinting for 3+ seconds triggers a tired panting sound (at most every 10 s, not while Fearful or Panicked). At level 5, about one blink in ten becomes a microsleep when not afraid: the eyes stay closed for about a second while the head drops, then snap open. Blinks and the head drop scale with the Distortion Effects slider; at 0 they are off. From Exhausted 3, the screen edges also darken with a neutral (not red) vignette at about 15/25/35% strength for levels 3/4/5. It fades in over a few seconds, droops slowly, and darkens just before and during each blink. When fear is also active, each color channel uses the stronger of the two vignettes instead of adding them, so the screen never goes black. The vignette scales with Distortion Effects, down to a 35% floor.
- Relieved scales with its level (1/2/3/4): fear recovers at 7/8/9/10 points per second instead of 5, aim sway and tremor fall by 20/30/40/50%, and PeakStamina regeneration rises by 5/10/15/20% (combined with the Exhausted and Pain penalties in a single modifier). Its effects end when the moodle disappears.
- When relief rises, the character lets out a sigh of relief (four recorded takes, louder at higher levels), followed by one or two slow calming breaths, and any Panicked breathing stops. The red fear vignette fades faster while relieved, and the view opens about 2% for a few seconds, scaled by FOV Effects. Whenever the heartbeat stops because fear dropped below Fearful, it keeps beating for about 7 seconds, slowing to about 50 bpm and fading out instead of stopping abruptly.

Survival and attribute penalties are applied by the server and do not apply in Creative or Spectator mode. TaCZ, PeakStamina, and Legendary Survival Overhaul integrations are optional; their respective effects activate when those mods are present.

## Test commands

Operators can force moodle levels for testing without changing health or distance traveled. The override affects the HUD and gameplay effects until cleared, the player dies, or the player disconnects. The underlying natural moodle continues updating.

- `/lm fear 0..3` — 1 Tense, 2 Fearful, 3 Panicked.
- `/lm pain 0..3` — force Pain independently of health.
- `/lm exhausted 0..5` — force Exhausted independently of travel distance.
- `/lm relieved 0..4` — force Relieved.
- `/lm status` — show current effective levels and identify forced values.
- `/lm clear` — remove all overrides; `/lm clear fear`, `pain`, `exhausted`, or `relieved` clears one.

`/lazarusmoodles` is an alias for `/lm`. Level 0 hides a moodle until the override is cleared. Commands require permission level 2 (cheats enabled in single player).

Install the built JAR in `mods` on both the server and every client. The icons are original generated pixel art stored in `assets/lazarusmoodles/textures/gui/moodles.png`. All mod text is English.

Download the ready-to-install JAR from [`dist/lazarusmoodles-1.10.0.jar`](dist/lazarusmoodles-1.10.0.jar), or build it with JDK 17: `gradlew build`.
